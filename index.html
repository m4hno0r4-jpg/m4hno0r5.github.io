(03)The Studio

Architecture Rooted in Intention.

Founded in Paris in 2009, MAISON ATELIER is a studio of architects, interior designers and craftspeople who believe the most enduring spaces are the most restrained.

We begin every commission by listening — to the site, its light, and the lives it will hold — then remove everything that does not belong, until only what is essential remains.
